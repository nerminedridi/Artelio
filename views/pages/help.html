Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection matter to us.
This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information while using our platform.

1. Information We Collect:

We collect basic account information such as your name, email address, profile image, and activity on the platform. This helps us personalize your experience and connect you with other artists, curators, and collectors.

2. How We Use Your Data:

Your information is used to manage accounts, process purchases, improve platform performance, and provide personalization. We analyze user behavior to enhance your experience and platform functionality.

3. Cookies & Analytics:

Cookies help us remember preferences, maintain login sessions, and analyze website traffic. These tools allow us to improve navigation, optimize platform user experience, and create a better browsing experience.

4. Security & Protection:

We apply security measures such as encrypted connections and secure payment systems to help protect your information. Access to personal data is restricted and monitored to maintain platform security.

5. Third-Party Services:

Certain services, including payment processing and analytics, may involve trusted third-party partners. These partners are obligated to keep your information confidential and use it only to perform their functions.

6. User Rights & Control:

Users may update account information, request data removal, or contact support regarding transparency and give users greater control over their personal information.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect platform improvements or legal requirements. Users will be notified of significant changes when necessary.
